Privacy Policy

Application: Loony  |  Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Loony collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related disaster alert & emergency GIS services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide real-time hazard detection, landslide risks, and emergency weather nowcasting, our application may collect the following information:

  • Precise & Approximate Location Data: Used to display radar rain overlays, compute proximity to hazardous zones (floods, flash floods, landslides), and generate emergency routing alternatives. Location data is processed on-device and against our secure API endpoints.
  • Device & Sensor Information: Device model, OS version, app performance logs, and network connection status to ensure reliable offline & online sync during natural emergencies.
  • User-Generated Reports (Optional): If you submit crowdsourced hazard or road obstruction reports, the report data (severity, optional notes, coordinates) will be stored to alert nearby citizens.

2. Location Data Usage & Permissions

Loony requests location access strictly to fulfill safety-critical functionalities:

  • Foreground Location: Pinpointing your position on high-resolution meteorological and terrain maps.
  • Background Location (if enabled): Delivering life-saving early warnings when you enter active landslide hazard zones or impending flash flood pathways. You can adjust or revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

The collected information is utilized solely to:

  • Deliver real-time rainfall, landslide warnings, and safe evacuation paths.
  • Improve predictive meteorological models and hazard zone mapping.
  • Diagnose app crashes and enhance application stability.
  • Prevent spam or fraudulent hazard reports.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only interface with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Mapping & Spatial Data: MapLibre, OpenStreetMap, and official meteorological data sources for basemap tiles and weather imagery.
  • Crash Analytics: Anonymized crash logs to troubleshoot device-specific issues.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide service functionality. Anonymous location queries for map tiles are ephemeral and not permanently stored linked to your identity.

If you have created an account or submitted hazard reports and wish to erase your data, you can request full account and data removal at any time using our Account & Data Deletion Portal.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately to remove it.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
