Google Play Data Safety

Request Account & Data Deletion

Application: Loony

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and data protection standards, users of Loony have full autonomy over their personal data and account records.

Warning: Account and data deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your saved preferences, hazard pins, and profile details will be permanently removed.

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion

You can instantly delete your account directly inside the mobile app:

  1. Open the Loony app on your device.
  2. Tap your Profile / Settings icon in the navigation drawer or top bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm your decision. All associated records will be immediately purged.

Option 2: Web-Based Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit your deletion request below. Our support team will process your request within 7 business days.

✓ Your request has been drafted. If your mail client didn't open automatically, please send an email directly to aravindsmailoff@gmail.com.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • User Profile & Authentication Data: Email, username, and authentication tokens.
  • Saved Locations: Custom pinned areas, bookmarked infrastructure, or evacuation points.
  • Submitted Reports: Any user-submitted road/landslide reports will be untracked and decoupled from your personal identity.
  • Notification Tokens: Push notification device tokens registered on our server.

What Data Is Retained?

No personal identifying data is retained after deletion. Fully anonymized, aggregated meteorological and spatial usage metrics (which cannot be linked back to any individual) may be maintained for statistical GIS accuracy.
